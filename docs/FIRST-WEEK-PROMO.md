# First Week of Free Promo — "Keeper of Small Fires" tee

Drafted by **SKALDR** (video) and **KALLARI** (social). All original, filmable on
a phone, $0. For each video: pick a **calm/cozy trending sound from TikTok's own
in-app library** (never upload copyrighted music). Add the product link in bio.

---

## 🎬 SKALDR — 3 short-form video scripts (15–20s each, vertical)

### Video 1 — "The keeper" (aesthetic / POV)
- **Hook (0–2s):** dim room, one candle lit. On-screen text: *"POV: you're the friend who always has a candle going."*
- **Beats:**
  1. (0–2s) Close on the candle flame, low light.
  2. (2–6s) You pull on the tee; soft natural light.
  3. (6–10s) Close-up of the chest text: *Keeper of Small Fires*.
  4. (10–15s) Coffee poured, blanket, book, candle in frame.
  5. (15–18s) You settle in, small smile.
- **On-screen text (end):** *"the shirt for people who keep small fires 🔥 — Emberkeep"*
- **Feel:** slow, warm, unhurried. No talking needed.

### Video 2 — "Cozy goblin starter pack" (relatable / humor)
- **Hook (0–2s):** *"things that make me feel like a cozy little woodland creature:"*
- **Beats:** fast satisfying cuts, ~1.5s each — steaming tea → lit candle → **the soft tee (hold it up / wear it)** → rain on the window → open book → fuzzy socks.
- **On-screen text:** number each item; make the tee the star (#3: *"this shirt 🥺"*).
- **End text:** *"Keeper of Small Fires · link in bio"*

### Video 3 — "Gift for the one who runs cold" (gift angle)
- **Hook (0–2s):** *"gift idea for the friend who's always cold and always has a candle lit 🕯️"*
- **Beats:**
  1. (0–3s) The tee folded neatly, warm light.
  2. (3–8s) Close-up of the text + fabric texture.
  3. (8–13s) Slip it into a gift bag / fold with a ribbon.
  4. (13–18s) Hand it over / hug reaction.
- **End text:** *"soft, cozy, made-to-order — Emberkeep"*
