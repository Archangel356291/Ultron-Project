## 4. Store policies (starting drafts — review before publishing)

- **Shipping:** "Made-to-order and shipped in 3–7 business days. Because our
  candles are glass, they ship with protective packaging; transit times vary by
  location." (Set **weight-based shipping** in Shopify so glass doesn't eat margin.)
- **Returns/Refunds:** "Because each candle is made to order, we accept returns
  only for items that arrive damaged or defective — send a photo within 14 days
  and we'll replace or refund it."
- **Privacy:** use Shopify's built-in Privacy Policy generator (Settings → Policies).
- **Candle safety (required on the product page + base label):** "Burn within
  sight. Keep away from things that catch fire and from children/pets. Trim wick
  to ¼″ before lighting. Never burn more than 4 hours at a time." (Printify adds a
  base safety label — verify it's on the product.)

## 5. Exact launch checklist (the free path — your clicks)

1. **Shopify free trial** at shopify.com (don't pay yet). Business name = your brand above.
2. **Theme:** Online Store → Themes → keep **Dawn**.
3. **Install Printify** (free) from the Shopify App Store; connect it to the store.
4. **Create 3–4 products** in Printify: pick a candle blank → upload a label made
   in **Canva** (free) to their template size (~3″×3″ for a 9oz jar) → set the
   retail prices above → **Publish to Shopify**.
5. **Policies:** Settings → Policies → generate Privacy; paste the shipping/returns
   drafts above; add the safety text to each product description.
6. **Shipping:** Settings → Shipping → add a weight-based profile for glass.
7. **Fulfillment:** in Printify, set order approval to **Automatic**.
8. **Payments:** Settings → Payments → Shopify Payments (needs your bank/ID — your step).
9. **Go live** only when you're ready to pay the intro plan (~$1–5). Until then it
   stays a finished draft at $0.
10. **Connect Odin's Eye:** Shopify → Settings → Develop apps → create app →
    read_orders scope → copy the `shpat_…` token → paste it + your store domain in
    the **SHOPIFY building → "Your API keys"** → hit **Sync Shopify orders**.
    Your profit/tax then tracks live on the Jarl's Stone.

## 6. Marketing with $0 (SPAMADR)

You can't afford ads and you don't need them to start:
- Post short vertical videos (TikTok/Reels): the candle glowing in a dim room, a
  close-up of the label, a "pack an order with me" clip. Scent can't travel through
  a screen — sell the *vibe* and the funny labels people screenshot.
- Post consistently (1×/day for 2 weeks), use trending sounds, reply to comments.
- Only spend on samples/ads once the store has made its own money first.
