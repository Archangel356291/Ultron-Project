# First Printful Product — Design-smith Brief (list it today, $0)

A text-only tee you can make in **Canva (free)** and list through **Printful
(already installed — no cost until someone buys)**. All text is original.

## The product (in Printful)
- Garment: **Bella+Canvas 3001** (soft, popular) *or* **Gildan 18000** (cheapest).
- Color: **Black** or **Heather Forest / Heather Deep Teal** (dark = light text pops).

## The design (front, center chest)
Three stacked, centered lines — cream text on the dark shirt:

```
        ── E M B E R K E E P ──          ← small, wide-spaced caps (your brand)
        Keeper of Small Fires            ← big serif headline
         light the long nights           ← small italic tagline
```

Pick **one** headline (all original):
- **Keeper of Small Fires**
- **Warm Halls · Long Nights**
- **Mind the Hearth**

Color for all text: cream **`#EDE6D2`**.

## Make it in Canva (free) — copy/paste these
1. canva.com → **Create a design** → **Custom size** → width **4500**, height **5400** px → Create.
2. If there's a white background, click it → Delete (you'll export transparent).
3. **Text** → **Add a text box** → type your headline (e.g. `Keeper of Small Fires`).
   - Font: **Playfair Display** (or **DM Serif Display**) · Size **~220** · Color **`#EDE6D2`**.
4. Add a text box **above** it → type your brand in caps: `E M B E R K E E P`
   - Font: **Montserrat** · Size **~70** · Letter spacing **300** · Color **`#EDE6D2`**.
5. Add a text box **below** → type the tagline: `light the long nights`
   - Font: **Playfair Display Italic** · Size **~90** · Color **`#EDE6D2`**.
6. (Optional) **Elements** → search **`flame line`** or **`line divider`** → filter **Free** →
   drop a small one between lines → recolor to `#EDE6D2`.
7. Select all → center it; keep everything inside the **middle ~60%** of the canvas.
8. **Share** → **Download** → **PNG** → tick **Transparent background** → **Download**.

## Put it on the shirt (Printful, free until sold)
1. Shopify admin → **Printful** → **Add product** → choose the tee + color(s).
2. Upload your PNG to the **Front** print area → drag to center chest. Printful shows the **base cost**.
3. Set **retail price ≈ 2–2.5× the base** (e.g. base ~$13 → sell **$26**).
4. Fill in the title/description (ask **KAUPMADR** for listing copy) → **Submit to store**.
5. In Shopify it appears under Products → **Publish**. Use Printful's auto-generated mockup photos (free).

That's a real product, live in your store, for **$0** — candles come later with Printify.
